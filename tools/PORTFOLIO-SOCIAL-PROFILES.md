## 3. How the Auto-Poster & Control Room Enforce Brand Routing

1. **Explicit Profile Tagging in Every Post:**
   Every entry in `campaign-30day-schedule.json` specifies `"target_profile": "Blue Seas Fishing (@blueseasfishing)"`.
2. **Visual Routing in the Control Room:**
   In your Control Room, every day card and copy block explicitly highlights:
   `📍 Post destination: [Platform] -> Blue Seas Fishing Profile`
3. **Dedicated Bio Link Copy Buttons:**
   The Control Room includes a **"🌐 Portfolio Profiles & Bio Links"** view where you can copy the exact bio link for any brand with one click.
