Striped bass vs. white bass: how to separate them

Do not use size alone. Young striped bass can overlap white bass in size, and hybrid striped bass can show a mixture of features.

Body shapeMore elongatedTypically deeper-bodied and more compressed

Photograph the decisive clues

  1. Take a full side view without covering the horizontal stripes.
  2. Photograph the tongue clearly enough to see the tooth-patch arrangement.
  3. Capture the rear edge of the gill cover.
  4. Record the lake, river or coastal location.

Hybrid striped bass complicate the picture

Hybrid striped bass often have broken or irregular horizontal stripes and a deeper body. Because hybrids may share the two-patch tongue trait with striped bass, use several features together and check which fish are stocked locally.

Regulations may treat these fish differently. Confirm the species with the local fisheries authority whenever harvest, possession or size limits depend on the identification.
