Questions

How does the free tier work?
Every visitor gets 3 AI identifications per day, free forever — counted per browser. A free account raises that to 5/day. Premium removes the cap entirely.
What is a "trip plan"?
Tell us where and when you're fishing; we cross-reference our 548-species database and build a target list with baits and the hotspot data from each species profile. It's live now in the Trip Planner — premium members can print or save any plan as a PDF.
How does the weekly bite brief work?
Every week we generate a fresh brief for your region from the same species database: what should be biting, with baits and season windows. It's shown live in the members area, and emailed to you weekly if you opt in there.
Can I cancel?
Anytime, from the billing portal linked in the members area. Your identifications stay available; daily limits resume.
Do you share my data?
No. See the privacy policy — your photos are used only to identify your fish.
