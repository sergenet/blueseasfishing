Sailfish vs. blue marlin: how to tell them apart

A sail versus a rounded fin: the name literally describes the difference.

Photograph these features

  1. Dorsal fin fully raised — the sail is unmistakable
  2. Full profile for build comparison
  3. Bill length relative to head
  4. Flank pattern — sailfish show scattered light dots

When it is not clear-cut

Atlantic and Indo-Pacific sailfish were long treated as one species and are now often split; the sail ID holds for both. Juvenile blue marlin can show tall dorsals — check the body depth.

Identification tools can be wrong. Confirm uncertain fish with the local fisheries authority before relying on the result for harvest or possession rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is the sail fin always raised?
No — it folds flat into a groove and raises when excited or feeding.
Which is the angler favorite?
Sailfish: high jumps, big numbers in season, and reliable catch-and-release action.
Can you keep them?
Most fisheries are strictly catch-and-release for billfish; check local rules.
