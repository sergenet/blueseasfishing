Fishing Basics: Knots, Rigs & First Trips

Learn 3 must-know knots, 3 simple rigs, and a “no-stress” checklist for your first few outings.

Three Knots That Cover 95%

  1. Improved Clinch — fast, great for hooks/lures. Steps: 5–7 wraps → back through the small loop → through big loop → moist → tighten.
  2. Palomar — super strong for braid/mono. Steps: double line → overhand knot → pass loop over lure → tighten.
  3. Loop Knot (Non-slip) — lets lures swing naturally (jerkbaits/topwater).

Three Simple Rigs (Freshwater)

  • Bobber + Hook for panfish & trout ponds.
  • Ned Rig 1/16 oz mushroom head + small stick bait.
  • Texas Rig weedless soft plastic for bass.

Three Simple Rigs (Salt/Brackish)

  • Popping Cork + Shrimp deadly in marshes/flats.
  • Jig + Paddle Tail slow roll along the bottom.
  • Carolina Rig egg sinker + leader for current.

Recommended Starter Gear

Medium-Fast Spinning Combo 8 lb Mono (Freshwater) 30 lb Braid (Inshore) 20 lb Fluorocarbon Leader Fishing Pliers

First-Trip Checklist

  • Local regs + license, barbless where required.
  • Polarized sunglasses, hat, water, sunscreen.
  • Camera/phone for fish ID and release photos.
