Recommended Fishing Gear

Versatile Spinning Combo

A 6½–7 foot medium-power spinning rod with a matching reel is a practical all-around choice for bass, trout, panfish, and light inshore fishing.

Look for: a comfortable grip, smooth drag, corrosion-resistant guides, and a line rating that matches your target fish.

Compact Tackle Kit

Start with hooks in a few useful sizes, split shot or sinkers, swivels, leader material, jig heads, and a small selection of soft plastics.

Look for: adjustable compartments, a secure latch, and enough space to keep wet or rusty gear separate.

Essential Fishing Tools

Fishing pliers, dedicated line cutters, a hook-removal tool, measuring tape, and a small first-aid kit cover the most common needs.

Look for: rust-resistant materials, a secure sheath or tether, and tools suited to the hook sizes you use.
