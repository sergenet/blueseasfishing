Florida reefs (US) Fishing Guide

South Florida and the Keys sit against the only living coral barrier reef in the continental US, and the reef line delivers: yellowtail, mangrove, and mutton snapper, black grouper, plus sailfish and dolphin just past the edge. It's the rare place where a light-spinning angler and a offshore troll can share the same charter.
