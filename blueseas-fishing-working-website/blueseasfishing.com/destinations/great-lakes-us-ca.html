Great Lakes (US/CA) Fishing Guide

The Great Lakes are an inland ocean with a stocked salmon and trout fishery to match — chinook, coho, steelhead, and lake trout over deep water, plus a walleye scene that anchors entire towns. When the lake is flat, the salmon trolling is as close to ocean fishing as fresh water gets.
