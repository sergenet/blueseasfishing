Southern US rivers Fishing Guide

The South's big rivers — the Mississippi system above all — produce a freshwater big-game list few anglers expect: 60-pound flathead and blue catfish, alligator gar, drum, and buffalo. Summer night fishing and winter trotline country, this is river fishing on an industrial scale.
