Licences & regulations — England

  • An Environment Agency rod licence is required on all canals.
  • Most canal stretches also need a Canal & River Trust permit or are covered by it — check the waterway before fishing.

Official sources: gov.uk/fishing-licences · canalrivertrust.org.uk
