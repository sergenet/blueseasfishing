How to read these charts: each bar's full width is the all-tackle world record. The blue fill is a typical catch; the red notch is a trophy benchmark — a fish most anglers remember for years. The record figures are widely reported all-tackle marks; the IGFA record database is the authoritative source — verify current records there before planning a record attempt. Average and trophy figures are working benchmarks from charter and tournament norms, not official statistics.
