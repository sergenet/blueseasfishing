World record vs average fish size

46 species across 5 families, each charted from the all-tackle world record down to the fish you actually catch. Some records are a lifetime away; others are closer than you think.

Bass & Sunfish

The everyday freshwater quarry. The gap between an average bluegill and the record book is smaller than you think — and the largemouth record has stood, tied, for decades.

  • Largemouth Bass — 22.4 lb record
  • Smallmouth Bass — 11.9 lb record
  • Spotted Bass — 10.3 lb record
  • White Bass — 6.8 lb record

Pike, Walleye & Rough Fish

Cool-water predators and river giants. The muskellunge record has survived 75 years of better gear and sonar; the alligator gar record is older than most living anglers.

  • Walleye — 25 lb record
  • Northern Pike — 55.1 lb record
  • Muskellunge — 67.5 lb record
  • Chain Pickerel — 9.4 lb record

Trout, Salmon & Catfish

Cold-water icons and the catfish that outgrow them. The Kenai chinook and the Buggs Island blue cat show how far a big river system can stretch a species.

  • Brook Trout — 14.5 lb record
  • Brown Trout — 42.1 lb record
  • Rainbow Trout — 48 lb record
  • Cutthroat Trout — 41 lb record

Inshore Saltwater

Bays, beaches and jetties. Red drum, halibut and cobia — the records here came from water you can reach in a bay boat or from shore.

  • Red Drum — 94.1 lb record
  • Black Drum — 113 lb record
  • Spotted Seatrout — 17.4 lb record
  • Snook — 53.6 lb record

Full inshore saltwater chart →

Offshore & Billfish

Bluewater legends. The 1979 Nova Scotia bluefin and the 1953 Chilean swordfish have outlived every 'unsinkable' record that followed them.

  • Wahoo — 184 lb record
  • Mahi-Mahi — 87.9 lb record
  • Yellowfin Tuna — 388.8 lb record
  • Bluefin Tuna — 1496 lb record

Full offshore & billfish chart →

Where the numbers come from: record weights are widely reported all-tackle marks — the IGFA is the authoritative source and the final word. "Typical catch" and "trophy size" are working benchmarks from charter, tournament and fishery-survey norms, rounded honestly. Verify records before planning an attempt.
