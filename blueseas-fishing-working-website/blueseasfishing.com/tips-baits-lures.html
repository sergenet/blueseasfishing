Freshwater Staples

  • Inline Spinner cast cross-current, moderate retrieve.
  • Plastic Worm (Senko) weightless or Texas rig, slow twitches.
  • Jig + Craw hop down ledges/wood for bass.

Inshore/Salt Staples

  • Gold Spoon steady pace over grass flats.
  • Paddle Tail + Jig slow roll near bottom.
  • Topwater Walker dawn/dusk, “walk the dog”.

Recommended Picks

Inline Spinners Senko-style Worms (5") Jig + Craw Gold “Fishing” Spoons Paddle Tails + Jig Heads Topwater Walkers

Retrieve Basics

  • Cold water: slow it down, pause longer.
  • Windy/choppy: louder/brighter baits often win.
  • Clear water: natural colors, subtle actions.
