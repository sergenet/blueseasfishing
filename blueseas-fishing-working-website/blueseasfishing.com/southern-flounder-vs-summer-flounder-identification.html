Southern Flounder vs. summer flounder: how to tell them apart

Both flatfish face right. Spot patterns and range do the talking.

Photograph these features

  1. Eyed side flat on the deck for spot count
  2. Tail edge profile
  3. Mouth open — fluke show larger teeth
  4. Location noted: the two coasts differ

When it is not clear-cut

Gulf anglers also meet gulf flounder; the 3-spot rule holds there too. Fin-ray counts are the lab-grade tiebreaker but the spot pattern is usually enough.

Identification tools can be wrong. Confirm uncertain fish with the local fisheries authority before relying on the result for harvest or possession rules.

Frequently asked questions

Which is bigger?
Summer flounder reach larger typical sizes in the Northeast; southern flounder grow big in Gulf passes.
Both "fluke"?
Northeast anglers call summer flounder "fluke"; southern flounder is the Gulf/Carolina flatfish.
Same baits?
Both take live finger mullet, mud minnows and soft plastics bounced along the bottom.
