Yellow Perch vs. white perch: how to tell them apart

Not actually cousins — a true perch versus a temperate bass that borrowed the name.

FeatureYellow PerchWhite Perch
FamilyTrue perch (Percidae)Temperate bass (Moronidae) — related to white bass
PatternBold dark vertical bars on gold-greenPlain silver-gray sides, faint bars at most
FinsOrange-red pelvic and anal finsPlain gray fins
SizeHand-sized; 8–12 in typical6–10 in typical; bigger in brackish rivers

Photograph these features

  1. Full flank for bars versus plain silver
  2. Pelvic and anal fin color
  3. First dorsal fin — spiny in both, but shape differs
  4. Note the water — white perch tolerate brackish water

When it is not clear-cut

In coastal rivers the two can share the net. White perch lack the deep spiny-dorsal silhouette and orange fins of yellow perch at any size.

Frequently asked questions

Is white perch really a perch?
It is closer to striped bass and white bass than to yellow perch.
Which schools tighter?
Yellow perch school tightly; white perch scatter in loose groups.
Best bait for each?
Both take worms and small minnows; yellow perch love small jigs tipped with spikes.
