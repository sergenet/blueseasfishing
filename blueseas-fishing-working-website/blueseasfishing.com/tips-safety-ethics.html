Safety, Handling & Ethics

Stay safe, respect the resource, and release fish in great shape.

Fish Handling

  • Wet hands, support belly; keep gills in water when possible.
  • Barbless or crimp barbs where required; use pliers for quick releases.
  • Quick photos; revive facing current until strong.

Personal Safety

  • PFD on boats/kayaks; wading belt with waders.
  • Watch tides, lightning, and surf; don’t turn your back on waves.
  • First-aid kit, sun protection, hydration.

Regulations & Ethics

  • Check local regulations/licensing every trip.
  • Respect closures, slot limits, seasons.
  • Pack out trash; share water courteously.
