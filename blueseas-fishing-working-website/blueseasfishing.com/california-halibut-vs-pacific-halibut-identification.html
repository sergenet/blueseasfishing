California Halibut vs. pacific halibut: how to tell them apart

A nearshore 10-pounder versus a deep-water giant — plus one scale check.

Photograph these features

  1. Full eyed-side profile
  2. Skin texture close-up on the flank
  3. Tail and girth with something for scale
  4. Depth/location note — it narrows the pair fast

When it is not clear-cut

Both are left-facing (eyed side left) — the California fish is one of the few left-eyed Paralichthys cousins, while Pacific halibut is right-eyed, a quick orientation check.

Frequently asked questions

Which side do they face?
California halibut can face either way; Pacific halibut eyes sit on the right side. Orientation is a legitimate clue here.
Can they be caught the same way?
Different games: California halibut stalk surf and bay flats; Pacific halibut is a deep-drop bait-and-jig fishery.
Which tastes better?
Both are premium white fish; Pacific halibut fillets from bigger fish are the classic fish-and-chips flake.
