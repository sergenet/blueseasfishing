## What you're offering (plain English)

You are offering other websites a free little box they can add to their own pages.
Their readers upload a photo of a fish they caught, and the box instantly tells them
what species it is. The AI part runs on OUR servers — the other site does nothing.

Decoding the pitch line:
- **"One line of HTML"** — to install it, they copy/paste 2 short lines of code into
  their page. That's the whole installation. No programmer needed, takes 2 minutes.
- **"We host everything"** — the AI, the 548-species database and the servers are all
  on our side. They never install, update or maintain anything, ever.
- **"No API key"** — they don't need to register, create an account, or get a developer
  key. Most embeddable tools make you sign up first; ours doesn't. Paste and done.
- **"Free (attribution link only)"** — they pay nothing. The only "price" is a small
  "Powered by Blue Seas Fishing" line inside the widget that links to our site.

What WE get out of it: every site that embeds the widget links to us (a backlink —
helps our Google rankings) and their visitors click through to our species pages
(traffic). We give them a free interactive tool; they send us readers and rankings.

## WHY BATCH 1 GOT NO REPLIES (post-mortem, 2026-09-28)

All 3 sends on Sep 25 went to generic `info@` addresses. That's the main problem:

1. **`info@` inboxes are black holes.** They're triaged for bookings/partners, nobody owns
   them, and cold pitches die there. Personal name + personal Gmail gets opened.
2. **"Free widget" in the subject reads as spam** when it comes from an unknown domain.
   Fix: lead with THEIR content and a 30-second demo, drop "free" from the subject.
3. **One-size body.** Captains get pitches constantly; the ones that land reference the
   exact species/photos on their own page.

Fixes applied to Batch 2 below: real personal emails, captain's name in subject AND body,
species named from their own site, single clean CTA, demo-first framing. Send Tue–Thu,
7–10 AM recipient time. One follow-up after 6 days, then stop.
