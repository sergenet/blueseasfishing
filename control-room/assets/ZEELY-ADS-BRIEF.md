## Budget (when YOU launch)

- **Phase 1 test (5–7 days):** $10/day total, spread across Ads A + B only.
  Kill rule: CPC over $0.60 or landing bounce over 75% → pause.
- **Phase 2 (if CPA ≤ $0.75/link):** scale to $20/day, add Ad C + D.
- **Never** boost the homepage; check UTMs in GA4 `zeely_q4_*` campaigns weekly.

## Compliance guardrails (Awin + FTC — non-negotiable)

1. Ads point ONLY at blueseasfishing.com pages, never at affiliate/retailer links.
2. recommended-fishing-gear.html currently carries NO affiliate links (its
   gear-recommendations.html predecessor's meta said "without affiliate sales
   pressure") — Ad E is compliant as-is. When Piscifun links are added later,
   put a visible FTC disclosure on that page BEFORE resuming Ad E
   (site-wide disclosure currently lives in privacy.html).
3. No "free gear", giveaway or sweepstakes claims (Awin bans incentivized traffic).
4. Claims in copy must be true: 548 species profiles, 124 destination guides, AI ID tool ✓.

## Zeely setup steps (app.zeely.ai — phone or Chrome desktop)

1. Sign in → **Create ad** / **New campaign**.
2. Choose goal: **Traffic** (website). Connect/authorize the Facebook + Instagram
   accounts when prompted (one-time).
3. Upload the creative for the ad you're building (this folder, both sizes —
   Zeely adapts them).
4. Paste business **name**: `Blue Seas Fishing`; **website** from the ad's landing
   page above (keeps the UTMs intact — Zeely keeps your query string).
5. Paste Primary text + Headline from this sheet; pick CTA **Learn More**.
6. Enter targeting from the section above; set budget from the section above.
7. Zeely generates variations — keep max 2 per ad, delete extras (cleaner test).
8. **Leave everything as Draft / Schedule. Do NOT press Launch.** Review, then
   launch when ready — that click starts Meta billing.
9. Order: Ad A, then B. Add C/D/E after the first read (5–7 days).
